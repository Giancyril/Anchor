# Getting Started Guide

## Creating Your Account
Visit https://app.company.com/signup and enter your email address and a secure password. You will receive a verification email within 2 minutes. Click the link in the email to activate your account.

## Setting Up Your Profile
After verifying your email, complete your profile by adding your name, company, and timezone. This ensures notifications are sent at the right time.

## Connecting Your First Integration
From the dashboard, click **Integrations** in the left sidebar. Select the tool you want to connect (Slack, Jira, GitHub, etc.) and follow the OAuth flow. Most integrations take under 2 minutes.

## Inviting Team Members
Navigate to **Settings ? Team ? Invite Members**. Enter the email addresses of your teammates and assign them a role: Admin, Editor, or Viewer. Invites expire after 72 hours.

## API Access
API keys can be generated under **Settings ? Developer ? API Keys**. All API requests must include the header: Authorization: Bearer <your_api_key>. Rate limit is 1,000 requests per hour on the Starter plan, 10,000 on the Pro plan.
