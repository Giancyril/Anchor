# Troubleshooting Common Issues

## I cannot log in to my account
1. Ensure Caps Lock is not on and try re-entering your password.
2. Click **Forgot Password** to reset via email.
3. If you use SSO, contact your IT administrator to verify your credentials.
4. Check our status page at status.company.com for any ongoing incidents.

## My integration is not syncing
1. Go to **Integrations** and click **Reconnect** next to the affected integration.
2. Revoke and re-grant OAuth permissions if prompted.
3. Check that your third-party account has the required permissions enabled.
4. Wait 5 minutes and trigger a manual sync from the integration settings.

## I am not receiving email notifications
1. Check your spam/junk folder.
2. Verify your email address is confirmed in **Settings ? Profile**.
3. Ensure notifications are enabled under **Settings ? Notifications**.
4. Add no-reply@company.com to your email whitelist.

## How do I export my data?
Navigate to **Settings ? Data ? Export**. Choose a format (CSV or JSON) and the date range. Exports are prepared within 30 minutes and emailed to your account address.
