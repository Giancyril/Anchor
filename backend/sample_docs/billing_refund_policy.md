# Billing and Refund Policy

## Overview
Our billing is handled monthly or annually depending on your plan. All payments are processed securely via Stripe.

## Refund Eligibility
Customers are eligible for a **full refund within 14 days** of their initial purchase or subscription renewal. After 14 days, refunds are issued on a **pro-rated basis** for the unused months remaining on an annual plan.

Monthly plans are non-refundable once a billing cycle has started.

## How to Request a Refund
1. Log in to your account dashboard.
2. Navigate to **Settings ? Billing ? Request Refund**.
3. Fill in the refund reason form and submit.
4. Our billing team will process your request within **3–5 business days**.

## Payment Methods
We accept all major credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, Amex), PayPal, and wire transfers for enterprise accounts. Wire transfers may take **5–7 business days** to clear.

## Subscription Upgrades and Downgrades
You can upgrade or downgrade your plan at any time. Upgrades take effect immediately and are charged pro-rata. Downgrades take effect at the start of the next billing cycle.
